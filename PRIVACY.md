# Privacy Policy for B-FAST Inspector

*Last updated: November 4, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **B-FAST Inspector** ("the Extension"), developed by **Marcelo Markus**, handles user information.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection

B-FAST Inspector does **not** collect, store, track, transmit, or sell any personal information or user data.

Specifically, the Extension does NOT collect:
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- Authentication credentials, passwords, tokens, or security keys.
- Financial or payment details.
- Health or medical information.
- Geolocation data or IP addresses.
- Browsing history, list of visited websites, or user search queries.
- Keystroke logging, mouse movements, or behavioral telemetry.

---

### 2. Local-Only Processing (Chrome DevTools)

The Extension operates exclusively inside the local Chrome Developer Tools panel (`chrome.devtools`):
- All network payload inspections and B-FAST binary decodings occur **100% locally in your browser's memory**.
- No network requests, headers, or decoded payloads are ever transmitted off your local device.
- All proof card generations (Canvas rendering) happen strictly in local client memory.

---

### 3. No Remote Code & No Third-Party Analytics

- B-FAST Inspector contains **zero external analytics tools**, zero advertising scripts, and zero tracking pixels.
- The Extension does not load or execute remote code; 100% of all scripts are bundled locally inside the extension package in compliance with Manifest V3 policies.

---
